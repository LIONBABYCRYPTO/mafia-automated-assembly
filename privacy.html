Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Information We Collect

Werewolf ("the Game") collects minimal information to function:

Player Name — a nickname you choose when joining a game. This is visible to other players during the game session.

Room Code — a randomly generated code used to join a game session.

Game Actions — votes, night actions, and role assignments are stored temporarily to facilitate gameplay and are deleted when the game ends or the room expires.

How We Use Information

All data is used solely for operating the game. No personal information is stored permanently, sold, or shared with third parties beyond what is required to display ads.

Cookies & Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about Google's advertising practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Data Retention

Game data (rooms, players, roles, actions) is stored temporarily in Cloudflare Workers KV with a TTL of 24 hours. No long-term data storage occurs.

Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare (CDN & Workers KV) and Google AdSense. Each service has its own privacy policy governing data handling.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the repository owner at github.com/LIONBABYCRYPTO.
